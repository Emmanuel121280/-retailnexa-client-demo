# RetailNexa Product Scope and Claims Checklist

**Purpose:** A comprehensive scope for the public marketing website and later phased SaaS development. This is NOT proof that modules are presently operational.

## Platform foundation
- Multi-tenant companies, business signup, email verification, branches, workspaces
- Super Admin, company administrators, staff, roles, permission checks and audit logs
- Subscription plans, entitlements, Paystack payments, plan changes and billing
- Settings, security, encrypted integrations, API/webhook processing, background jobs, reports
- Universal dashboard and dashboard builder; customizable industry workspaces

## Retail / inventory / operations
- Product catalogue, universal identification, SKU/barcode, IMEI tracking, selling units and variants
- Stock intake, transfers, adjustments, stock movement, low-stock notifications
- POS, registers, cash and other tenders, receipts, refunds/returns, sales reports
- Purchasing, procurement, suppliers, expenses, deposits, ledgers
- Customers, CRM, credit/customer ledger, self-service customer portal, storefronts
- Production workflows, accounting, payroll, financial reports and profitability

## AI / commerce / integrations
- AI business assistant, financial-control AI, product intelligence, marketing AI
- Social sales, WhatsApp Cloud API integration, AI sales assistant, bot-to-human handover
- Webchat, outbound webhook delivery, payment integrations, notification workflows
- Public catalogues, product discovery, multi-merchant marketplace and loyalty programme

## Industry roadmap
- Restaurants: menus, tables, kitchen tickets, ingredients, delivery/takeaway
- Salons and services: services catalogue, bookings, calendars, reminders
- Schools/training: enrolment, tuition, attendance, guardians and reports
- Hotels/hospitality: rooms, reservations, check-in/out, housekeeping, billing
- Logistics: trucks, drivers, dispatch, trips, fleet maintenance, fuel, GPS integration
- Property: properties, tenants, leases, rent, maintenance
- Professional services: projects, contracts, appointment scheduling, invoices

## Device protection
- Barcode/IMEI tracking and device guard ideas. Any theft locking/control capability must only be marketed as available once real, legally appropriate device/platform integrations and end-to-end tests substantiate it.

## Claim rules
- Mark confirmed live functions as Available only after reproducible tests.
- Mark partially integrated/untested functions as In development.
- Mark planned industries and marketplace capabilities as Roadmap until validated.
- Marketing website features (catalogue/selector/demo chat) are website interactions, not production business workflows.
- Avoid promising guaranteed income, universal device locks, guaranteed WhatsApp access or feature readiness unsupported by evidence.
